Dear Member:

Northstar Health Plan has completed its review of the prior authorization
request submitted by your provider for the service listed above. After review
by a licensed clinical reviewer, we have determined that the requested service
is not medically necessary at this time and coverage is DENIED.

Reason for determination: Based on the information submitted, the clinical
criteria in Northstar Medical Policy MP-RAD-014 (Advanced Imaging of the
Spine) were not met. The policy requires documentation of at least six (6)
weeks of conservative therapy (such as physical therapy, medication, or
activity modification) prior to advanced imaging, unless red-flag symptoms are
present. The records received document approximately three (3) weeks of
conservative therapy and do not document red-flag symptoms.

This is not a decision about whether you need the service. You and your
provider may still decide to proceed; however, the plan will not cover the
cost of the service if performed without an approved authorization.

YOUR RIGHT TO APPEAL
You have the right to request an internal appeal of this decision. Your appeal
must be received within 180 calendar days from the date of this notice. You
may submit written comments, documents, records, and other information
relating to your claim. You may request, free of charge, copies of all
documents relevant to this decision, including the medical policy cited.

If your condition is urgent and waiting for a standard appeal could seriously
jeopardize your life, health, or ability to regain maximum function, you or
your provider may request an EXPEDITED APPEAL by calling (555) 010-7788. We
will decide expedited appeals within 72 hours.

Your provider may also request a peer-to-peer discussion with our medical
director by calling (555) 010-7790 within 14 calendar days of this notice.
